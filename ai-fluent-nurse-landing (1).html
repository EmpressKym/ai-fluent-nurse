How the lessons work

Short video lessons

About 5 to 10 minutes each, so you can learn in the gaps of a busy week.

Copy-and-paste prompts

Templates you can adapt to the documentation formats your facility uses.

Quick knowledge checks

A few questions at the end of each lesson to lock in the safety habits.

Every example uses invented scenarios. No real patient information, ever.

Clear limits, on purpose

  • AI drafts. You review, edit, and approve everything that goes in the chart.
  • AI should not guide real-time decisions during a code or rapid response. The course covers preparation beforehand and documentation afterward.
  • This is education only, not medical, legal, or compliance advice. Your facility's policies and your scope of practice always come first.
